# SIG Algothon 2026 Research

Private research repository containing my personal EDA, experiments,
strategy development and submission history for the SIG Algothon.

## Project objective

Develop a systematic trading strategy for 50 stocks and the ALGO
instrument under the competition's position and trading constraints.

## Current strategy

The current strategy combines three engines:

1. Pairs trading
2. Cross-sectional Ridge regression
3. Short-horizon autocorrelation signals
